---
title: "ADR 0005 amendment of 2026-10-26: a heartbeat dated more than one TTL ahead is also stale"
---

Amended 2026-10-26: a lock is stale when its heartbeat lies more than one TTL from now in either
direction. The decision made a lock stale once its heartbeat was older than the configured TTL, so
staleness was `now - heartbeat > ttl`, which is negative for a heartbeat dated in the future.

**Why.** The holder writes the heartbeat from its own clock. A holder whose clock ran fast, or a
record copied from another machine, can leave a heartbeat hours or years ahead of every other
session's clock. Under the old rule that lock never aged past the TTL: `lock acquire --take-stale`
refused it for as long as the skew lasted, and only `lock break` freed it. A heartbeat more than one
TTL ahead is now stale, so `--take-stale` takes it over as it would an old one; a heartbeat less
than one TTL ahead stays live, so ordinary skew between machines does not make a fresh lock stale.

**No negative ages.** The `locked` error reports the lock's age and heartbeat age as zero, never a
negative duration, when either time lies ahead of now, as `capcom prime` and the status bar
already do.
