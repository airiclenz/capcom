---
title: "Knowledge: the committed, shared body of facts about the repository and rules it adopts, held as Notes"
---

The committed, shared body of facts about the repository, and rules it adopts, that agents consult
on demand: conventions, gotchas, the domain glossary, decision records and the reasons behind
them. It holds **Notes**, which people and agents may edit by hand as well as through capcom.
_Avoid_: memory, docs, wiki
