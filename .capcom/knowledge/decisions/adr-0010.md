---
title: Knowledge holds a glossary and decision records as Note trees
---

Status: Accepted.

Knowledge may hold a repository's glossary and decision records as Note trees, one Note per term and
one per section; Notes may be edited by hand, ids may be one word, citations resolve as ids, and
Notes gain the `retired` and `superseded-by` keys. Read `adr-0010-decision` for the shape and the
migration method, then `adr-0010-rejected` for indexing in place, plain split files, summary Notes
and the other options weighed.
