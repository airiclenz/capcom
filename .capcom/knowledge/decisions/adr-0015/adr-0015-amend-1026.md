---
title: "ADR 0015 amendment of 2026-10-26: closed is a fourth tab name in ui.tab and ui.selected"
---

Amended 2026-10-26: `capcom ui` gained a fourth tab, Closed, which lists the closed issues, so
`closed` is a fourth tab name. `ui.tab` takes `ready`, `issues`, `knowledge` or `closed`, and
`ui.selected` keys the id selected on the Closed tab under `closed`, as it does the other three. A
stored Closed selection that is gone or no longer closed selects the first row without a word, as
on the other tabs; an older capcom that does not know `closed` opens Ready and drops the key on its
next save, as for any unknown key.
