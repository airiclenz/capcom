---
title: "Base set: the Notes capcom ships in its binary, which capcom know seed adds to a store"
---

The **Notes** that capcom ships embedded in its binary: one root **Note** and the Notes below it,
each a rule that holds in any repository. `capcom know seed` adds the ones a store lacks. A seeded
**Note** carries two **Labels** that mark it, and is the repository's own from then on.
_Avoid_: default Notes, template, starter kit
