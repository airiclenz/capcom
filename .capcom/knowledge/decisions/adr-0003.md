---
title: Statuses are configurable, and behaviour reads only their category
---

Status: Accepted.

A repository declares its own issue statuses, each mapped to one of five fixed categories, and every
rule in capcom reads the category, never the status name. Read `adr-0003-decision` for the rule and
the bd failure behind it, then `adr-0003-rejected` for a fixed set and free-form statuses.
