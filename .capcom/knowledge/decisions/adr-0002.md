title: Ids are chosen slugs, and old names live on as aliases
superseded-by: [adr-0010]
---

Status: Accepted; amended 2026-10-12.

Every id is a short kebab-case slug the author chooses, with no project prefix, and a renamed id
lives on as an Alias that every lookup resolves. ADR 0010 later lowered the floor from two words to
one. Read `adr-0002-decision` first, then `adr-0002-amend-1012` for the one-word floor, then
`adr-0002-rejected` for generated ids and kept bd ids.
