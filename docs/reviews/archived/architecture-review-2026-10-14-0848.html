Problem. The membership rules (who holds an id or Alias, repeated-id subtrees, orphans, prefix resolution) exist in tree, again in index Go, again in index SQL; comments admit the copying, no test checks agreement, and alias reading has already drifted.

Solution. One module decides membership and resolution; the index persists its verdicts and answers queries over them.

  • Locality: claim rules in one module
  • Edge cases tested once
  • Index tests shrink to persistence
  • Drift between copies becomes impossible
Touches ADR-0001 (directory tree is the hierarchy) and ADR-0002 (ids and Aliases) — consistent with both; it changes who implements them, not what they say.

  • rawfm as a tolerant reader — real separate responsibility (only the fence scan duplicates).
