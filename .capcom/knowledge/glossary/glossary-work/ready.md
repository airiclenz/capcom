title: "Ready: a todo Issue, not a Standing Parent, with every Dependency closed, no open children and no waiting or dropped ancestor"
---

An **Issue** whose **Status** is in the `todo` category, whose **Dependencies** are all `done` or
`dropped`, which has no open children, which has no `waiting` or `dropped` ancestor and which is
not a **Standing Parent**.
_Avoid_: available, unblocked
